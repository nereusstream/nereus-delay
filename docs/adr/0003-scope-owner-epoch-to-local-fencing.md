# Scope `ownerEpoch` to local fencing

Nereus Delay uses `ownerEpoch` to reject stale claims, publish admission, state updates, callbacks, and checkpoint publication inside the service; it does not claim that arbitrary Kafka or Pulsar Brokers will reject an old Owner's in-flight requests. Lease loss stops new local work and turns unresolved Publish Attempts into `UNCERTAIN`; an unordered `BEST_EFFORT` baseline policy may retry those attempts and therefore preserve at-least-once delivery at the cost of duplicates, while strict ordered delivery holds for evidence or an explicit domain boundary. Broker-specific fencing, transactional receipts, and deduplication remain optional capability Profiles, while `delayMessageId + generation`—not `ownerEpoch`—is the logical idempotency key.
